Who We Are

45 Years Building Namibia's Future

Windhoek Consulting Engineers has maintained a strong reputation as a pioneer in the industry for over four decades, delivering excellence from planning and design through construction and management.

Our expertise spans transportation, urban and rural infrastructure, bulk services, structural design, mechanical systems, electrical engineering, green hydrogen, oil and gas, renewable energy, and mining engineering.

Mission
To be Namibia's most trusted and innovative local engineering partner, delivering world-class solutions on every project.
Vision
Engineering a sustainable, prosperous Namibia for current and future generations through technical excellence.
